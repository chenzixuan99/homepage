About Me

Hi! I am Zixuan Chen (陈子璇). I received my Ph.D. degree from the School of Computer Science and Technology, Nanjing University (NJU) in March 2026, advised by Prof. Yang Gao and Assoc. Prof. Jing Huo. I have joined the School of Intelligent Science and Technology, Nanjing University (Suzhou Campus) as a Postdoctoral Researcher (Yuxiu Young Scholar).

Previously, I received my B.E. and M.E. degrees from Soochow University, School of Computer Science and Technology, under the supervision of Prof. Zongzhang Zhang. From Nov. 2024 to Nov. 2025, I was a visiting student at the National University of Singapore (NUS), working with Prof. Lin Shao.

Research Interests

My research focuses on Robot Learning, Reinforcement Learning, and Imitation Learning for embodied agents. I am particularly interested in three complementary directions, one target problem supported by two core capabilities:

  • Long-horizon Embodied Tasks: Leveraging prior knowledge from vision-language models (VLMs) to build agentic frameworks for task decomposition, skill composition, and closed-loop execution in long-horizon manipulation, contact-rich dexterous manipulation, mobile manipulation, and humanoid loco-manipulation.
  • Spatial Intelligence: Grounding actions in the physical world through generalizable 3D spatial perception and reasoning (e.g., geometric relations, affordances).
  • Embodied World Models: Learning generative models of environment dynamics to enable predictive planning and scalable data generation.
